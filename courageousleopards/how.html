How It Works: Courageous Leopard's Cartography

Choose, Wander, Notice

Pick a route that matches your time and comfort level. Each paper kit includes a folded map, a short story prompt, and a few observation questions; there is no app, timer, or score to keep up with.

  1. Read the route notes and choose a public path that feels comfortable.
  2. Follow the map at your own pace, pausing whenever a clue catches your attention.
  3. Share one detail you noticed, then return the route card or pass it to a friend.

Go with a companion if you like, check weather and accessibility before setting out, and turn back whenever you need. The best ending is getting home with a new story about a place you already knew.
