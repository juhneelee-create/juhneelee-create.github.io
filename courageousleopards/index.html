A Field Guide for the Pleasantly Curious

Courageous Leopard's Cartography is a pocket expedition bureau: a tiny kiosk for story-led walking adventures around Charlotte. Each Pocket Expedition pairs a printed route with a handful of gentle clues that invite you to notice the patterns, sounds, and stories hiding in ordinary places.

Choose a route, take a short walk, and see what catches your eye. These are easygoing self-guided outings for curious neighbors, visiting friends, and anyone who wants a fresh reason to take the long way home.
