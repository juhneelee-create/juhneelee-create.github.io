Start here

What is Brawlhalla?

Brawlhalla is a free-to-play platform fighting game from Blue Mammoth Games. Players choose a Legend, fight on floating stages, and try to knock opponents off the arena. As damage builds, a fighter becomes easier to launch away.

Fight for the stage

Move, jump, dodge, and attack while watching your position. A well-timed hit can send an opponent flying past the edge.

Choose your style

Each Legend has a distinct look and a set of two weapon types. Pick up weapons during a match and adapt your attacks to the situation.

Legends and players

Who can play?

Anyone can jump into Brawlhalla. The roster of playable fighters is called the Legends; each one brings a different visual identity and combination of weapon types to the fight.

Find a Legend you enjoy

Try different Legends in training and pay attention to their weapons, movement, and feel. Comfort and curiosity matter more than picking a so-called perfect main.

Play together

Practice with friends, team up, or face other players. Matches are a chance to learn from different play styles, not only to chase a win.

Pick a match

When should you play?

Whether you want a laid-back match for fun or a more competitive test of your skills, Brawlhalla has a way to play that fits the moment. Enjoy casual rounds at your own pace, or jump into ranked matches when you are ready to face a tougher challenge.

Play just for fun

Casual matches are a relaxed way to hang out, try a new Legend, or enjoy a few rounds without worrying about your rank.

Bring on the competition

  • Try ranked when you want a serious challenge.
  • Practice a weapon or matchup between games.
  • Play with a team and work toward a shared win.

Choose your arena

Where do battles happen?

The fight takes place on a stage made of platforms and open space. Learn how the layout affects movement: a narrow ledge, a high platform, or a long drop can change your next decision.

Modes for different groups

  • One-on-one matches focus on individual decisions.
  • Team battles reward coordination with a partner.
  • Free-for-all matches add more opponents and chaos.

Read the stage

Keep track of the edge, platforms, and your route back after a launch. Staying aware of the arena is just as important as watching your opponent.

Learn the basics

How do you play?

Start with movement, attacks, and recovery. In a platform fighter, staying alive means managing the space around you as well as choosing when to strike.

Three things to practice

  1. Move and jump to control your position.
  2. Use light and heavy attacks with deliberate timing.
  3. Save jumps or a dodge to recover toward the stage.

Know your weapon

Weapons have different attack ranges and rhythms. Learn a few reliable moves, watch for a weapon pickup, and avoid repeating the same predictable attack.

More than a knockout

Why play Brawlhalla?

The fun comes from quick decisions: finding an opening, protecting your position, and adapting to what the other player does. Even a close loss can show you what to practice next.

Every round is different

Weapon choices, spacing, movement, and opponent habits combine in new ways. The same attack will not solve every situation.

Improve one decision at a time

Try a new weapon, practice recovering, or notice when you overcommit. Small adjustments can make the next match more rewarding.
